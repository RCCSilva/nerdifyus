# The draft
Level: intermediate
Applies to: rules (S30); real example: the 2026 draft (S31, S32)

## What it is
- "Each of the 32 clubs receives one pick in each of the seven rounds of the NFL Draft" [S30].
- 2026 draft: April 23–25 in Pittsburgh; round 1 Thursday, rounds 2–3 Friday, rounds 4–7 Saturday; 257 picks in total [S31]. (7 × 32 = 224, plus 33 compensatory picks [S32] = 257.)

## The order
- Teams that missed the playoffs get slots 1–20: worst record picks 1st, best record 20th [S30].
- Playoff teams get 21–32 by how far they went: wild card losers 21–24, divisional 25–28, conference championship 29–30, Super Bowl loser 31, champion 32 [S30].
- Ties: strength of schedule first, then head-to-head, common games, … coin toss [S30].
- Real example: the Las Vegas Raiders held the No. 1 pick in 2026 [S31].

## On the clock
- Time per pick: round 1, 8 minutes; round 2, 7; rounds 3–6, 5; round 7, 4 [S30].
- Miss it and you can still pick later, but the next team may take your player [S30].

## Trading picks
- Teams may trade picks at any time before and during the draft [S30]. Compensatory picks can be traded too: Detroit traded its 2026 special compensatory pick to Jacksonville [S32].

## Compensatory picks
- Up to 32 extra "compensatory free agent" picks, placed at the end of rounds 3–7, for teams that lost more or better free agents than they signed; at most four per team [S30].
- "Compensatory free agents are determined by a formula based on salary, playing time and postseason honors" [S32].
- 2026: 33 compensatory picks to 15 clubs, including one special pick (2020 CBA amendment that rewards a club when a minority coach or executive it developed is hired as a head coach or primary football executive elsewhere), awarded to Detroit [S32].

## Who can be drafted
- Players must be "out of high school for at least three years and must have used up their college eligibility before the start of the next college football season" [S30].
- Underclassmen have "seven days following the NCAA National Championship Game to declare" [S30].
