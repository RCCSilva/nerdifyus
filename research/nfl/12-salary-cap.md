# The salary cap
Level: advanced
Applies to: the 2020 CBA (S33); 2026 figures (S35)

## What it is
- "The Salary Cap is the same amount for each Club" [S33 Art. 13 §1, p.123]. "No Club may have a Team Salary that exceeds the Salary Cap" [S33 Art. 13 §2, p.123].
- 2026: $301.2 million per club, the first time above $300 million; 2025: $279.2M; 2024: $255.4M; 2022: $208.2M [S35].

## Where it comes from
- The players' share (Player Cost Amount) is built from league revenues: 55% of league media, 45% of NFL Ventures/postseason, 40% of local revenues, and for 2021–2030 it must land between 48% and 48.5% of projected revenues [S33 Art. 12 §6(c)(i)–(ii), p.112].

## Minimum spending
- Each team must spend in cash at least 90% of the cap over each multi-year period (2021–23, 2024–26, 2027–30); shortfalls are paid to the players [S33 Art. 12 §9, p.117]. League-wide: 95% [S33 Art. 12 §8, p.117].
- The 90% team floor applies from 2021; for 2017–2020 it was 89% [S33 Art. 12 §9(a)–(b), p.117].
- League-wide: "Guaranteed League-Wide Cash Spending of 95% of the Salary Caps … multiplied by the number of Clubs" for each period [S33 Art. 12 §8(b), p.117]. A league shortfall is paid to the players who were on a club roster during the period, per NFLPA allocation, reduced by any team shortfall payments [S33 Art. 12 §8(d), p.117].

## Signing bonus proration
- A signing bonus is "prorated over the term of the Player Contract (on a straight-line basis…), with a maximum proration of five years" [S33 Art. 13 §6(b)(i), p.126].
- Example (illustrative): $25M bonus on a 5-year contract → $5M a year on the cap, paid up front in cash.

## Dead money (acceleration)
- Player released or traded on or before June 1: all unamortized signing bonus counts in the current league year [S33 Art. 13 §6(b)(ii)(1), p.127].
- After June 1: the future years count fully at the start of the next league year [S33 Art. 13 §6(b)(ii)(2), p.127].
- Each team may designate up to two releases before June 1 to be treated as if after June 1 ("post-June 1") [S33 Art. 13 §6(b)(ii)(1), p.127].
- Example (illustrative): the $25M/5-year bonus, released after 2 seasons with $15M left → before June 1: $15M dead money this year; after June 1: $5M this year, $10M next year.

## Carrying over room
- A team can carry unused cap room into the next league year by notifying the league the day after its last regular-season game [S33 Art. 13 §6(b)(v), p.129].
