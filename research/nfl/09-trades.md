# Trades
Level: intermediate
Applies to: the 2020 CBA (S33) and the 2026 calendar (S34)

## What can be traded
- Player contracts and draft picks. Teams may trade picks at any time before and during the draft [S30]; compensatory picks too (Detroit traded its 2026 special pick to Jacksonville) [S32].

## When
- Trading runs from the start of the league year until the trade deadline. 2026: "All trading ends for 2026 at 4:00 p.m., New York time" on November 10; it resumes when the next league year begins, in March [S34].

## What happens on the salary cap (signing bonus)
- A signing bonus is spread over the contract on the cap (max 5 years) [S33 Art. 13 §6(b)(i), p.126].
- If a player is traded, "the Team Salary of the player's new team will not include any portion of the signing bonus" [S33 Art. 13 §6(b)(ii)(3), p.127].
- For the old team, the unamortized signing bonus accelerates: traded on or before June 1 → all of it counts in the current league year; after June 1 → the future years' amounts count fully at the start of the next league year [S33 Art. 13 §6(b)(ii)(1)–(2), p.127].
- The traded player's salary (Paragraph 5) for the year moves with him: the new team includes the amounts it pays under the contract [S33 Art. 13 §5(a), p.124].
- Example (illustrative, applying the rule): $20M signing bonus on a 5-year deal = $4M per year. Traded before June 1 after 2 seasons → the old team takes the remaining $12M this year ("dead money"); the new team takes none of the bonus.
